{% comment %} "Pages to extract" control for the PDF upload forms (worksheets, homework, ai_import). Three modes, the way a print dialog offers them: All pages the default — nothing is excluded Range from/to boxes, e.g. 5 to 20 Custom a free list, e.g. 5, 6, 8, 9-11 Whichever mode is chosen, the form still posts ONE field — `page_selection`, a print-dialog spec string — so the server contract and the validated parser in worksheets/page_selection.py are unchanged. "All pages" posts an empty string, which is also what a browser with no JS posts, so the no-JS fallback is the old "extract everything" behaviour rather than a broken form. Optional context: ps_accent — Tailwind colour stem for focus rings, e.g. "blue-500". Defaults to the worksheets/ai_import theme's "primary". {% endcomment %}

Pages to extract (optional)

Skip a cover or instruction sheet at the front and a marking scheme at the back. Pages you leave out are never read, so they don't use AI tokens or your monthly page quota.

to Leave “to” blank to read to the end of the PDF.

Single pages and ranges, separated by commas — 5, 6, 8, 9-11. Use 2- for page 2 to the end.
