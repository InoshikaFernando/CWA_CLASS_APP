{% comment %} Monthly AI page allowance — the meter shown above every upload form that spends pages (homework, worksheets, AI import). Takes the dict returned by billing.page_quota.quota_status() as `page_quota`. Renders nothing at all when the school isn't metered (an unlimited tier), so it never shows an empty 0/0 bar. A school with NO AI module is metered at zero — it cannot read a PDF with AI on any of the three screens — and gets the purchase panel instead of a meter, since a 0/0 bar and a "resets on the 1st" would both be lies. `page_quota.no_allowance` is the flag for that case; `page_quota.exhausted` means the school HAD an allowance and spent it, which needs different words and a different link. The warning threshold is billing.page_quota.WARN_RATIO (75%) and arrives as `page_quota.warn` — don't re-derive it from the percentage here, or the banner and the code that actually refuses an upload can drift apart. {% endcomment %} {% if page_quota.no_allowance %}
