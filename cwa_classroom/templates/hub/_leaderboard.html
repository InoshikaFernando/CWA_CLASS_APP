{% comment %} Student leaderboards — one tab per scope (their school, then everyone), each with a podium, the student's own place, and a line of encouragement. Rendered twice on the hub: inline as a card that stays on the page, and inside the once-a-day pop-up. Both copies are on the page at once, so every id here is namespaced with `prefix` (passed by the includer) — two elements sharing an id would make the pop-up's tabs drive the card's panels. The school tab comes first: a field a student can realistically climb beats one where the honest answer is "you are 400th". An individual student belongs to no school and gets the system-wide board alone, with no tab strip at all. The place badges are numerals in gold/silver/bronze discs rather than the 🥇🥈🥉 emoji: a device without a full emoji font falls all three back to one identical glyph, which makes the podium unreadable — and a numeral is what a screen reader should say anyway. Context: `standings` (list of rewards.services.Standing), `prefix` (str). {% endcomment %}
