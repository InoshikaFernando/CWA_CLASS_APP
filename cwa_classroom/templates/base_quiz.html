{% comment %} The progress-art panel sits BESIDE the quiz, not under it. Below the questions it was out of sight on anything but the shortest page — a child had to scroll away from the question they were answering to see the picture they were earning, which is most of the point of it gone. So when a view supplies `progress_art`, this becomes two columns: the quiz on the left, the picture pinned on the right. A quiz that has NOT opted in is untouched — same narrow reading column, and the wrappers below collapse to classless divs. That matters: the times-tables and basic-facts drills share this template. Sticky, not fixed, so it stops under the topbar and travels no further than the quiz column is tall. `lg:self-start` keeps the flex item from stretching to the row height, which would leave sticky no room to move inside its own box. The panel is FIRST in source so a phone — where this is a plain block, not a flex row — gets it above the quiz; `lg:order-2` puts it back on the right once the columns appear. {% endcomment %}
