Most often answered wrong

Top {{ wrong_rate_top_n }} by wrong answers ÷ all answers, from quiz attempts. A rate far above its neighbours' is usually the answer key, not the children.

Each row carries what the children answered against what the question accepts: many of them writing the same thing points at the key or the accepted format rather than at the children. Drawing, graphing and drag-and-drop questions keep no readable answer, so they show as “no answer recorded” rather than being left out.

Questions answered fewer than {{ wrong_rate_min_attempts }} times are left out — one child answering once is 100% and tells you nothing. Retired questions are left out too.
